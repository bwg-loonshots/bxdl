BXDL development package — selected Java runtime notices

provided by Eclipse Adoptium. The complete upstream JRE Home files are
preserved without jlink minimization. File contents are unchanged; packaging
normalizes filesystem permissions to 0644/0755.

Java/OpenJDK and Eclipse Temurin marks remain their respective owners' marks.

The exact runtime file inventory and archive identity are recorded in
 docs/runtime-provenance.json. Upstream metadata and SBOM are preserved in
 docs/runtime/supplier-metadata.json and licenses/temurin-supplier-sbom.json.
The supplier SBOM JRE archive hash differs from the released archive hash;
see docs/runtime/observed-runtime.json. The cause has not been established.
licenses/SBOM.json is a BXDL-generated runtime-only file/component inventory,
not a complete product dependency SBOM. Engine/native/Rust dependency notices
and complete product supply-chain review remain release work.

Matching OpenJDK source and Temurin build material are identified by
 docs/runtime/source-supply.json. The development acceptance retains both
archives locally. Customer delivery must include or provide equivalent access
to corresponding source material together with the binary; recording upstream
links alone does not establish completed source delivery.
